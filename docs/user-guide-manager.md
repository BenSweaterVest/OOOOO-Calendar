## Manager Overview

### What's Different for Managers?

As a manager, you have access to:

✅ **Approval Workflow**: Approve/reject time-off requests via Teams
✅ **Team Calendar View**: See entire team's schedule at a glance
✅ **Manager Approvals Screen**: In-app approval management
✅ **Approval History**: Complete audit trail of all decisions
✅ **Team Reporting**: Absence patterns and team availability
✅ **Override Capabilities**: Edit team member schedules if needed
✅ **All Staff Features**: Everything regular users can do

## Approving Time-Off Requests

### How the Approval Process Works

```
Employee submits → You receive Teams → You approve → Employee
  OOO request       notification      or reject     notified
                                                      ↓
                                                   Outlook
                                                   calendar
                                                   updated
```

### Receiving Approval Requests

**Via Microsoft Teams (Primary Method)**:

When an employee submits a time-off request, you'll receive:

1. **Teams Notification** with approval card
2. **Push Notification** on mobile (if enabled)
3. **Badge** on Teams app icon

**In the OOOOO Calendar App**:

You'll also see pending approvals in the app's "Manager Approvals" screen.

---

### Approving a Request in Teams

**Step-by-Step**:

1. **Open the Teams Notification**
   - You'll see an "Approval" card in Teams
   - Or find it in the "Approvals" app in Teams

2. **Review Request Details**:
   ```
   Time Off Request from John Doe

   Employee: John Doe
   Request Type: Vacation
   Start Date: December 24, 2025
   End Date: December 26, 2025
   Total Days: 3
   Submitted: November 15, 2025 10:30 AM

   Employee Comments:
   "Family holiday plans, traveling to visit relatives"
   ```

3. **Make Your Decision**:
   - Click **"Approve"** to approve the request
   - Click **"Reject"** to reject the request

4. **Add Manager Comments** (Optional but Recommended):
   - Add context for your decision
   - If approving: "Approved - enjoy your time off!"
   - If rejecting: "Sorry, we have 3 people already out that week. Can you consider alternative dates?"

5. **Submit Your Response**:
   - Click **"Submit"**
   - The system processes your decision automatically

**What Happens Next**:

✅ SharePoint record updates with your decision
✅ Employee receives Teams notification
✅ If approved: Outlook calendar event created automatically
✅ Approval history recorded for audit trail

---

### Approving in the OOOOO Calendar App

**Alternative Method** (in addition to Teams):

1. Open OOOOO Calendar app
2. Click **"Manager Approvals"** screen
3. See list of pending requests
4. Click on a request to view details
5. Enter manager comments (optional)
6. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"**
7. Request is processed

**Note**: You can use either Teams approvals OR the in-app method - both work!

---

### Decision-Making Considerations

**Before Approving**, consider:

✅ **Team Coverage**: How many people are already out?
✅ **Business Needs**: Any critical deadlines or meetings?
✅ **Fairness**: Ensure equitable distribution of time off
✅ **Notice Period**: Was adequate advance notice provided?
✅ **Past Requests**: Check employee's time-off history

**Tips**:

- **Check Team Calendar** before deciding
- **Communicate** if you need more information
- **Be Consistent** with approval criteria
- **Respond Quickly** to show respect for employees' time

---

### Handling Edge Cases

**Request for Same-Day or Next-Day**:
- For emergencies (sick leave): Approve retroactively
- For vacation: Generally should be rejected (insufficient notice)
- Use judgment based on circumstances

**Too Many People Out**:
- Reject and suggest alternative dates
- Communicate team coverage concerns
- Work with employee to find compromise

**Extended Leave (>5 days)**:
- May require additional HR approval
- Check organizational policies
- Coordinate with other managers if needed

**Overlapping with Critical Events**:
- Explain business need in rejection comments
- Offer alternative dates if possible

## Override & Edit Capabilities

### When to Override Schedules

**Valid Reasons**:
- Employee forgot to update status
- Emergency changes needed
- Correcting errors
- Retroactive approvals (sick leave)

**Important**: Communicate with employee before making changes!

### How to Edit Team Member Schedules

1. Open **Team Calendar**
2. Click on an employee's date cell
3. Select new status
4. Add reason for change (optional)
5. Save

**Or**:

1. Go to SharePoint **StaffSchedule** list
2. Find the item to edit
3. Change status or details
4. Save

**Tracked Changes**:
- All edits are logged in approval history
- Your name appears as the person who made the change
- Ensures accountability and audit trail

## Best Practices for Managers

### 🚀 Respond Quickly to Requests

**Target**: Respond within **24 hours**

- Employees are planning around these requests
- Quick responses improve morale and trust
- Set aside time daily to check approvals
- Enable Teams mobile notifications

---

### 💬 Always Add Comments

Even if just a quick note:

- **Approvals**: "Approved - have a great vacation!"
- **Rejections**: "Can't approve due to 3 people already out. Try week of Dec 17?"

Benefits:
- Shows you're engaged
- Provides context for decisions
- Helps employees understand reasoning
- Creates better record for future reference

---

### 📅 Check Team Calendar Before Deciding

**Every Time**:

1. Open team calendar
2. Check the requested dates
3. See who else is out
4. Consider coverage needs
5. Then make your decision

Prevents:
- Approving too many overlapping requests
- Coverage gaps
- Unfair distribution of time off

---

### 📊 Review Approval History Monthly

**Set Calendar Reminder**:

Once per month:
1. Review approval history
2. Check approval turnaround time
3. Look for patterns or issues
4. Identify employees with zero time off (burnout risk!)
5. Plan for upcoming busy periods

---

### 🤝 Communicate Proactively

**Don't rely only on the app**:

- Talk to employees about upcoming time off
- Discuss coverage plans in team meetings
- Share team calendar during planning sessions
- Set expectations about blackout dates (if any)

---

### ⚖️ Be Fair and Consistent

**Establish Clear Criteria**:

- Document your approval guidelines
- Apply same standards to everyone
- Explain decisions when asked
- Be transparent about coverage needs

Avoid:
- Playing favorites
- Arbitrary rejections
- Inconsistent standards
- Lack of communication

---

### 🔒 Protect Employee Privacy

**Remember**:

- Sick leave is private medical information
- Don't share details without permission
- Use general terms in public discussions
- Keep personal information confidential

## Manager FAQs

### Q: Can I approve requests retroactively?

**A**: Yes! This is useful for emergency sick leave. Employee submits request when they're able, and you can approve it for past dates. The system will record the actual occurrence date.

---

### Q: What if I accidentally approved/rejected a request?

**A**:
1. Go to SharePoint **StaffSchedule** list
2. Find the item
3. Change status back to "OOO-Pending" or correct status
4. Contact employee to explain
5. If needed, resubmit approval or make manual correction

---

### Q: Can I see requests before they're submitted?

**A**: No - employees submit directly. However, encourage employees to discuss major time-off plans with you in person/Teams before submitting formal requests.

---

### Q: How do I handle conflicting requests?

**A**:
1. First come, first served (generally fair)
2. Or consider business need and context
3. Work with employees to find compromise
4. Document your reasoning in comments

---

### Q: What if too many people requested the same week?

**A**:
1. Approve based on business need
2. Consider submission order
3. Discuss alternatives with later requesters
4. Plan coverage with remaining staff
5. Set expectations early about popular periods

---

### Q: Can I set blackout dates when no one can take time off?

**A**: The system doesn't enforce blackout dates automatically, but you can:
1. Communicate blackout dates to team
2. Reject requests during those periods
3. Add note in rejection explaining blackout
4. Update **SystemSettings** to document blackout dates

---

### Q: How do I handle extended leave (FMLA, parental leave, etc.)?

**A**: For extended leave:
1. Use the OOOOO Calendar for day-to-day tracking
2. Coordinate with HR for official extended leave process
3. May need to create multiple OOO entries
4. Document in employee file per HR requirements

---

### Q: Can I delegate approval authority when I'm on vacation?

**A**: This depends on system configuration:
1. Work with IT to set up delegate approver
2. Or handle urgent approvals before leaving
3. Communicate who has coverage for approvals
4. Consider using Out of Office in Teams to redirect requests

---

### Q: What reports should I review regularly?

**A**:
- **Daily**: Pending approval requests
- **Weekly**: Team calendar for next 2 weeks
- **Monthly**: Approval turnaround time and patterns
- **Quarterly**: Absence trends and coverage analysis

---

### Q: How do I handle a pattern of last-minute sick leave?

**A**:
1. Document the pattern using approval history
2. Discuss with employee privately
3. Follow HR policies for attendance issues
4. Keep records in approval system for documentation
5. Consult with HR if needed

---

### Q: Can I approve time off for myself?

**A**: No - you cannot approve your own requests. Submit your OOO requests to your manager (or designated approver).

## Advanced Manager Features

### Setting Team Expectations

**Recommended**: Create a team agreement on:

- Minimum advance notice (3 days minimum, more for long vacations)
- Blackout dates (if any)
- Maximum number of people out simultaneously
- Onsite expectations (e.g., Tuesdays and Wednesdays preferred onsite)
- Process for emergency time off

**Document this** and share with team!

---

### Handling Holidays

**Organization Holidays**:
- Coordinate with HR for official holiday calendar
- Consider adding to **SystemSettings** as reference
- Employees don't need to request time off for official holidays
- System can be configured to show holidays automatically

---

### Planning Team Events

**Use Team Calendar** to:
- Find dates when most/all people are available
- Plan team meetings and events
- Avoid scheduling on days with low attendance
- Coordinate with other teams if needed
