## Updating Your Daily Status

### What is Daily Status?

Your daily status indicates whether you'll be working **Onsite** (in the office) or **Offsite** (remote/from home) on any given day.

### How to Update Your Status

**Quick Update from Home Screen**:

1. Open the OOOOO Calendar app
2. On the home screen, you'll see "Today's Status"
3. Click one of the quick options:
   - 🏢 **Onsite** - Working at the office today
   - 🏠 **Offsite** - Working remotely today
4. Your status is updated immediately!

**Update Future Days**:

1. Go to "My Schedule" screen
2. Click on any upcoming date
3. Select status (Onsite/Offsite)
4. Click Save

**Update Multiple Days**:

1. Go to "My Schedule" screen
2. Click "Bulk Update" (if available)
3. Select date range
4. Choose status
5. Click Apply

### Status Types

| Status | What It Means | Icon |
|--------|---------------|------|
| **Onsite** | Working at the office | 🏢 |
| **Offsite** | Working remotely/from home | 🏠 |
| **OOO-Pending** | Time-off request awaiting approval | ⏳ |
| **OOO-Approved** | Approved time off | ✅ |

## Requesting Time Off

### When to Request Time Off

Submit a time-off request for:
- ✅ Vacation days
- ✅ Sick leave
- ✅ Personal days
- ✅ Any day you won't be working

**Important**: Submit requests **at least 3 days in advance** (configurable by your organization).

### How to Submit a Request

**Step 1: Open Request Form**

From the home screen, click the **"Request Time Off"** button.

**Step 2: Fill Out the Form**

1. **Select Dates**:
   - **Single Day**: Choose one date
   - **Multiple Days**: Select start and end dates

2. **Choose Request Type**:
   - Vacation
   - Sick Leave
   - Personal Day
   - Other

3. **Add Comments** (Optional):
   - Add any notes for your manager
   - Example: "Family vacation" or "Medical appointment"

**Step 3: Submit**

1. Review your request details
2. Click **"Submit Request"**
3. You'll see a confirmation message
4. Your request is now **OOO-Pending** (awaiting manager approval)

### Example Request

```
📅 Dates: December 24-26, 2025 (3 days)
📝 Type: Vacation
💬 Comments: "Holiday travel with family"
```

### What Happens Next?

1. **Manager Receives Notification**: Your manager gets an approval request in Microsoft Teams
2. **Manager Reviews**: Manager can approve or reject with comments
3. **You Get Notified**: You receive a Teams notification with the decision
4. **Calendar Updated**: If approved, the time-off is added to your Outlook calendar automatically

### Tracking Your Request

**View Request Status**:

1. Go to "My Schedule" screen
2. Look for requests in "Request History" section
3. Status will show:
   - ⏳ **OOO-Pending** - Waiting for approval
   - ✅ **OOO-Approved** - Approved by manager
   - ❌ **OOO-Rejected** - Not approved (see manager comments)

## Viewing Your Schedule

### My Schedule Screen

The "My Schedule" screen shows your personal calendar and request history.

**To View Your Schedule**:

1. Click **"My Schedule"** from the home screen
2. You'll see:
   - **Calendar View**: Visual representation of your schedule
   - **Upcoming Entries**: Next 30 days
   - **Request History**: All submitted requests and their status

**Filter Your Schedule**:

- By date range: Select start and end dates
- By status: Show only Onsite, Offsite, or OOO days
- By request type: Filter vacation, sick leave, etc.

**Export Your Schedule** (if available):

1. Click "Export" button
2. Choose format (Excel, CSV)
3. Download file for your records

## Viewing Team Calendar

### Why View Team Calendar?

The team calendar helps you see:
- Who else is out of office
- Team availability for meetings
- Onsite vs. offsite days for coordination

### How to View Team Calendar

1. Click **"Team Calendar"** from the home screen
2. You'll see a grid showing:
   - **Rows**: Team member names
   - **Columns**: Dates
   - **Color-coded blocks**: Status for each person/day

**Color Legend**:
- 🟢 **Green** = Onsite
- 🔵 **Blue** = Offsite
- 🟡 **Yellow** = OOO-Pending (awaiting approval)
- 🟣 **Purple** = OOO-Approved (out of office)

**Navigate the Calendar**:

- Click **Previous** / **Next** arrows to change weeks/months
- Click **Today** to return to current date
- Switch between **Weekly** and **Monthly** views

**Search for Specific People**:

1. Use the search box
2. Type a team member's name
3. Calendar filters to show only that person

## Canceling Requests

### Cancel a Pending Request

If you need to cancel a time-off request that hasn't been approved yet:

1. Go to **"My Schedule"**
2. Find the request in **"Request History"**
3. Click on the request
4. Click **"Cancel Request"** button
5. Confirm cancellation
6. The request status changes to "Cancelled"

**Note**: You can only cancel **pending** requests. Once approved, ask your manager to help cancel.

### Cancel an Approved Request

If you need to cancel an already-approved request:

1. Contact your manager directly
2. Manager can update the request status
3. Outlook calendar event will be removed automatically

## Tips & Best Practices

### 📅 Plan Ahead
- Submit time-off requests **as early as possible**
- Check the minimum advance notice requirement (usually 3 days)
- Popular holiday periods may have limited approval capacity

### 👀 Check Team Calendar First
- Before requesting time off, check who else is out
- Coordinate with team to ensure coverage
- Avoid requesting time when too many people are already out

### 💬 Add Context to Requests
- Use the comments field to explain your request
- Helps manager make informed decisions
- Example: "Doctor's appointment in the morning, will be back after lunch"

### ✅ Keep Status Updated
- Update your daily status (Onsite/Offsite) regularly
- Helps team coordinate meetings and collaboration
- Takes less than 30 seconds!

### 📱 Use Mobile App
- Install Microsoft Teams on your phone
- Access OOOOO Calendar on the go
- Approve time-off requests from anywhere

### 🔔 Enable Notifications
- Keep Teams notifications enabled
- You'll know immediately when manager responds
- Never miss important updates

## FAQs

### Q: How far in advance should I request time off?

**A**: At least **3 business days** (this may vary by organization). The earlier, the better for planning purposes!

---

### Q: What if my manager doesn't respond to my request?

**A**:
1. Check that your manager received the Teams notification
2. Send a follow-up message in Teams
3. If urgent, contact your manager directly
4. Approval requests have a 7-day timeout (configurable)

---

### Q: Can I request time off for today?

**A**: Generally **no** - the system requires advance notice. For emergency sick leave, contact your manager directly and update the system when possible.

---

### Q: What's the difference between Offsite and OOO?

**A**:
- **Offsite** = Working remotely/from home (you're working)
- **OOO** (Out of Office) = Not working (vacation, sick, etc.)

---

### Q: Can I see past history of my requests?

**A**: Yes! Go to "My Schedule" → "Request History" and you'll see all past requests, approvals, and rejections.

---

### Q: What if I made a mistake in my request?

**A**:
- If **pending**: Cancel the request and submit a new one
- If **approved**: Contact your manager to help modify it

---

### Q: Will my Outlook calendar update automatically?

**A**: Yes! When your manager approves a time-off request, it's automatically added to your Outlook calendar as an "Out of Office" event.

---

### Q: Can I request partial days or half days?

**A**: This depends on your organization's configuration. If not supported in the app, discuss partial days with your manager directly.

---

### Q: Why was my request rejected?

**A**: Check the manager's comments on your request. Common reasons:
- Too many people already out on those dates
- Not enough advance notice
- Business needs or deadlines
- Talk to your manager for clarification

---

### Q: I'm sick today. What should I do?

**A**:
1. Contact your manager immediately (call, text, Teams message)
2. When you're able, submit a sick leave request in the app
3. Your manager can approve retroactively if needed

---

### Q: Can I update someone else's schedule?

**A**: No, you can only update your own schedule. Managers have additional permissions to view and manage team schedules.

---

### Q: The app isn't loading. What should I do?

**A**:
1. Refresh the Teams app (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
2. Close and reopen Microsoft Teams
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact IT support if issue persists
